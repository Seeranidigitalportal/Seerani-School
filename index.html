Stay safe online

Five rules every student can follow today.

Never share your OTP or passwordNot with friends, not with callers who say they are from a bank or a game.
Stop before you forwardCheck the source first. One shared lie can hurt a family.
Free prize? It is a scamReal prizes do not ask you to pay or click a strange link.
Bullying is not a jokeDo not reply. Take a screenshot and tell a teacher or parent.
Keep your private life privateDo not post your home address, school timings, or family photos publicly.
